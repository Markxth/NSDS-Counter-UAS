\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Handles point cloud preprocessing for the C-\/\+UAS Li\+DAR system. 

The \doxylink{classlidar_1_1_preprocessing_engine}{Preprocessing\+Engine} is responsible for cleaning raw point clouds before further processing. This includes range and region-\/of-\/interest filtering, downsampling, outlier removal, and ground removal.
