\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
Handles the guidance logic for the C-\/\+UAS GNC system. 

The \doxylink{classgnc_1_1_guidance_engine}{Guidance\+Engine} is responsible for receiving target packages and determining what the turret should aim at. The \doxylink{classgnc_1_1_guidance_engine}{Guidance\+Engine} must also determine what the ballistic effects of the current projectile package will be, and calculate the necessary offsets to hit the chosen target.
