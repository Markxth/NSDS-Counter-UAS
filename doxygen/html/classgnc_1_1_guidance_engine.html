Detailed Description

Handles the guidance logic for the C-UAS GNC system.

The GuidanceEngine is responsible for receiving target packages and determining what the turret should aim at. The GuidanceEngine must also determine what the ballistic effects of the current projectile package will be, and calculate the necessary offsets to hit the chosen target.
